# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 2026-11-15 | **Applies to:** the GH-ListRollup browser extension (all versions)

GH-ListRollup **collects no data**.

- It runs only on `github.com`.
- It sends network requests only to GitHub, and only to load the contents of a folder you choose to
  expand. Those requests go out from your browser with your existing GitHub session, exactly as
  GitHub's own pages do.
- It has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or remote configuration, and it loads
  no remote code.
- Your display preferences are stored in your browser's extension storage and may sync through your
  browser account, if you have enabled that.
- If a future version offers an optional GitHub token, the token will be stored only on the device
  where you enter it. It will be sent only to `api.github.com`, and only on your behalf.

Nobody, including the developer, receives any information about you, your repositories or your
browsing.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/doublegate/GH-ListRollup/issues>.
